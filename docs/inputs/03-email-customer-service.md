Just to add our perspective. The thing customers ask for most is being able to cancel
right up until it ships. That is the actual complaint. Nobody has ever complained about
a fourteen day limit, they complain that they ordered something yesterday, it has not
shipped, and they still have to phone us.

So from where I sit the rule should just be: if it has not shipped, you can cancel it.

Also, whatever we do, customers need to be told it worked. At the moment they get
nothing until an agent emails them. Email is the minimum. Some of the team think we
should text as well but that is not my call and I do not know what it costs.
